Manifest recall feed

Signed snapshots of U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission (CPSC) recall
data for the Manifest Android app, which checks product photos against
recalls on the phone. The app reads v1/latest.json, verifies the ECDSA
signature of the manifest it points to and the SHA-256 of every file.

Data: CPSC Recalls API (saferproducts.gov); recall photos from cpsc.gov.
Not affiliated with or endorsed by CPSC.
